# Psychology Experimental Methods — locked project contract

## Locked design
- Three-condition between-subjects design.
- Condition 1: barrier + continuous manual secondary task.
- Condition 2: barrier + hand still.
- Condition 3: no barrier + hand still.
- All groups hold a pen with the dominant hand.
- Primary comparison: 1 vs 2.
- Control comparison: 2 vs 3.
- 1 vs 3 is not a core hypothesis.
- Not a full 2x2 design.
- Primary DV: total reading-comprehension score containing factual and inferential items.
- Primary hypothesis is non-directional / two-tailed.
- p > .05 must not be interpreted as equivalence or proof of no effect.
- Behavioral differences do not prove a cognitive-resource mechanism.
- Barrier is a control/design element, not proof of a mechanism.
